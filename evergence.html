Evergence

Civilisational continuity and open-ended evolution: a provisional philosophical, scientific and mathematical research framework for the continued survival and evolution of humanity and other life. Thesis work rather than software, kept beside the two civic documents written in the same voice.

Where the claims stand

A framework about survival is easy to misread as a promise. This one labels its own material and publishes the labels with the work, so a reader always knows which register a sentence is in.

Established mathematics

Balanced ternary, Euler's formula, roots of unity, infinite product spaces, Gödel's incompleteness theorems. Results that stand on their own; none is a physical theory of civilisation.

Established physics

Thermodynamics, the observed expansion of the universe and the standard cosmological model, within its tested domain; with it the established economics of scarcity, discounting arithmetic and public goods, held as constraints rather than objectives.

Unsettled physics

The ultimate fate of the universe, the nature of dark energy and whether new physics could alter the outcome. Open questions, treated as open.

Speculative construction

Ternary states on arbitrarily many dimensions; evolution as new degrees of freedom; evolutionary stages as extensions of a formal system; transitions that preserve a civilisation. Research language, labelled as such.

Survival with evolution is the principal ambition, not expansion for its own sake; the framework states what it does not propose as plainly as what it does: no prescribed purpose for all life, no compulsory immortality, no mandate to expand without limit, no claim that current physics already permits escape from heat death, no architect with unquestionable authority. No mathematical elegance, by itself, establishes physical reality.

Civic documents in the same voice

Two earlier documents that apply the same structural reading to governance and to the constraints an AI system must treat as hard. Written before Evergence; its ethics and architects documents carry the same commitments forward.

HA Human-AI Coexistence Standard ↗ Survival-first guardrails for AI systems able to affect the world: four non-negotiable constraints (human survival and public health, ecological stability, human agency and legitimate governance, information integrity) with objectives optimised only inside them. Ships as a standard, an agent prompt block, engineering requirements and a review checklist. RD Resilient Democracy ↗ A structural upgrade to representative governance, not a replacement for it: human sovereignty final; power scoped, temporary and removable without violence; competence informing policy while citizens keep legitimacy; transparency by default; every policy measurable and revisable; no algorithmic rule.
